Privacy Policy

Animal Earth  ·  awoak games

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information Animal Earth collects (and doesn't collect) when you use the app.

Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. Animal Earth does not require you to create an account or sign in.

Data Stored on Your Device

Animal Earth saves your game progress locally on your device. This includes:

  • Which animals you have discovered
  • The animals currently in your zoo
  • The date your game was last played

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

Analytics & Tracking

Animal Earth contains no analytics, no advertising networks, and no third-party tracking SDKs. We do not track your behaviour in or out of the app.

Children's Privacy

Animal Earth is designed for all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third-Party Services

Animal Earth does not integrate any third-party services that collect data.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update this page with a new effective date. We do not anticipate collecting personal data in the future.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at support@awoak.com.

Effective date: May 2026

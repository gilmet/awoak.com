Frequently Asked Questions

My progress disappeared

Your zoo is saved locally on your device. Progress can be lost if you delete and reinstall the app, or restore your device without restoring app data. We recommend keeping automatic device backups (iCloud or iTunes) enabled.

An animal isn't appearing after I answered correctly

Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, send us a message below and describe what happened — which animal, and roughly when.

The app crashes or won't open

Make sure your device is running iOS 14 or later. Force-quit the app and reopen it. If crashes continue, email us with your device model and iOS version.

I found a question with a wrong answer

We take accuracy seriously. Please email us with the question text and what you believe the correct answer is — we'll investigate and update it.

Didn't find your answer? We typically reply within 1–2 business days.
